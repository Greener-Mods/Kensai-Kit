This is a WEIDU mod thus currently is compatible with other mods.

  1 - Extract the contents of the zip file into your override folder

  2 - Run WeiDU and install

KENSAI KIT DESCRIPTION

KENSAI: A Kensai is a warrior who has been specially trained to become one with <PRO_HISHER> weapon. They are deadly and fast and are trained to fight without encumbrance, some considering armor the crutch of the martially unskilled.

Advantages:
- Begins with a +2 bonus to Armor Class.
- Begins with a +2 bonus to Movement Rate.
- Begins with a +1 bonus to Weapon Speed Factor.
- May use the Kai ability once per day. Gains one use at level 1 and an additional use every 4 levels thereafter.

KAI: For 1 round, all successful melee attacks deal maximum damage.

- 2nd level: Becomes immune to fear and morale failure.
- 3rd level: Gains a +1 bonus to hit and damage rolls every 3 levels.
- 4th level: Gains a +1 bonus to Weapon Speed Factor every 4 levels.

Disadvantages:
- May not use missile weapons.
- May not wear armor or helmets.
- May not wear gauntlets or bracers.
- May not dual-class or multi-class.
- Alignment restricted to lawful.
